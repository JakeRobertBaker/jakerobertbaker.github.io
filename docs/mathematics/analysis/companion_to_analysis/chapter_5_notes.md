# Chapter 5 Notes

Note exercise 5.19 gives us key results and notation:

$$
\begin{align*}
a_j^{+} & \coloneqq
\begin{cases}
a_j & \text{ if } a_j >0 \\
0 & \text{ if } a_j \leq 0
\end{cases}
,
& a_j^{-} & \coloneqq
\begin{cases}
- a_j & \text{ if } a_j < 0 \\
0 & \text{ if } a_j \geq 0
\end{cases}
\end{align*}
$$

Therefore

$$
\begin{align*}
\lvert a_j \rvert = a_j^{+} + a_{j}^{-}
\\
a_{j} = a_{j}^{+} + a_{j}^{-}
\end{align*}
$$

Let

$$
\begin{align*}
S_n & \coloneqq \sum_{j=1}^{n} a_j,
& \lvert S \rvert_n & \coloneqq \sum_{j=1}^{n} \lvert a_j \rvert \\
S_{n}^{+} & \coloneqq \sum_{j=1}^{n} a_{j}^{+},
& S_{n}^{-} & \coloneqq \sum_{j=1}^{n} a_{j}^{-} \\
\end{align*}
$$
