Theorem 1.2.1 The Axiom of Archimedes

Section titled “Theorem 1.2.1 The Axiom of Archimedes”

The result is trivial in Q\mathbb{Q}Q. The theorem is proving it in R\mathbb{R}R.

This theorem proves that there is no ℷ>0\gimel>0ℷ>0 in R\mathbb{R}R such that,

1n>ℷ\frac{1}{n} > \gimeln1​>ℷ for all nnn   ⟺  \iff⟺ ℷ\gimelℷ is smaller than all strictly positive rationals.

Suppose there exists such a 0<ℷ<1n0<\gimel< \frac{1}{n}0<ℷ<n1​ for all nnn.

Then by preservation of non strict inequality (Lemma 1.6 (vii)) 0<ℷ≤l=00<\gimel \leq l = 00<ℷ≤l=0 which contradicts the axiom of Archimedes.

If the theorem was true in Q\mathbb{Q}Q but not in R\mathbb{R}R we would have this exotic ℷ\gimelℷ.
