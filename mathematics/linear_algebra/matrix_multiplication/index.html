Then, concisely,

[ABCD][X1X2]=[AC]X1+[BD]X2\begin{bmatrix} A & B \\ C & D \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} X_1 \\ X_2 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} A \\ C \end{bmatrix} X_1 + \begin{bmatrix} B \\ D \end{bmatrix} X_2[AC​BD​][X1​X2​​]=[AC​]X1​+[BD​]X2​

with dimensions,

[ABCD][X1X2](p+q)×(p+q)(p+q)×l=[AC]X1(p+q)×pp×l(p+q)×l+[BD]X2(p+q)×qq×l(p+q)×l\begin{aligned} & \begin{array}{cc} \begin{bmatrix} A & B \\ C & D \end{bmatrix} & \begin{bmatrix} X_1 \\ X_2 \end{bmatrix} \\ (p+q)\times(p+q) & (p+q)\times l \end{array} \\ &= \begin{array}{c} \begin{array}{cc} \begin{bmatrix} A \\ C \end{bmatrix} & X_1 \\ (p+q)\times p & p\times l \end{array} \\ (p+q)\times l \end{array} + \begin{array}{c} \begin{array}{cc} \begin{bmatrix} B \\ D \end{bmatrix} & X_2 \\ (p+q)\times q & q\times l \end{array} \\ (p+q)\times l \end{array} \end{aligned}​[AC​BD​](p+q)×(p+q)​[X1​X2​​](p+q)×l​=[AC​](p+q)×p​X1​p×l​(p+q)×l​+[BD​](p+q)×q​X2​q×l​(p+q)×l​​

[Y1Y2][ABCD]=Y1[AB]+Y2[CD]\begin{bmatrix} Y_1 & Y_2 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} A & B \\ C & D \end{bmatrix} = Y_1 \begin{bmatrix} A & B \end{bmatrix} + Y_2 \begin{bmatrix} C & D \end{bmatrix}[Y1​​Y2​​][AC​BD​]=Y1​[A​B​]+Y2​[C​D​]
