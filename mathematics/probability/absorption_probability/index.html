Absorption Probability

In a Markov chain, every state i∈Ii \in Ii∈I is either transient or recurrent. All absorbing states are recurrent, but not all recurrent states are absorbing. In most absorption problems the recurrent states are the absorbing states.

Canonical Form

Section titled “Canonical Form”

When the state space is partitioned into transient states ItI_tIt​ and absorbing states IrI_rIr​, the transition matrix takes the block form

P=[QR0I]P = \begin{bmatrix} Q & R \\ 0 & I \end{bmatrix}P=[Q0​RI​]

where QQQ is the sub-stochastic matrix of transitions among transient states and RRR records transitions from transient states into absorbing states.

Fundamental Matrix

Section titled “Fundamental Matrix”

Transient and recurrent states are characterised by

i is recurrent  ⟺  ∑n=0∞pi,i(n)=∞i is transient  ⟺  ∑n=0∞pi,i(n)<∞  ⟹  pi,j(n)→0.\begin{align*} i \text{ is recurrent} &\iff \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} p_{i,i}^{(n)} = \infty \\ i \text{ is transient} &\iff \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} p_{i,i}^{(n)} < \infty \implies p_{i,j}^{(n)} \to 0. \end{align*}i is recurrenti is transient​⟺n=0∑∞​pi,i(n)​=∞⟺n=0∑∞​pi,i(n)​<∞⟹pi,j(n)​→0.​

(I−Q)−1=I+Q+Q2+⋯(I - Q)^{-1} = I + Q + Q^2 + \cdots(I−Q)−1=I+Q+Q2+⋯

Absorption Probability

Section titled “Absorption Probability”
Theorem · Absorption Probability#

Let iii be a transient state and kkk an absorbing state. The probability of being absorbed into the recurrent class of kkk (via state kkk) is

Pi(absorbed by k)=(NR)i,k.\mathbb{P}_i(\text{absorbed by } k) = (NR)_{i,k}.Pi​(absorbed by k)=(NR)i,k​.
Proof

Conditioning on successive steps through the transient states,

Pi ⁣(enter recurrent class of k via state k)=∑t=0∞∑j∈ItPi(Xt=j) pj,k=∑t=0∞∑j∈It(Pt)i,j Rj,k=∑j∈It[∑t=0∞(Qt)i,j]Rj,k=∑j∈It(I−Q)i,j−1 Rj,k=(NR)i,k.\begin{align*} &\mathbb{P}_i\!\left(\text{enter recurrent class of } k \text{ via state } k\right) \\ &= \sum_{t=0}^{\infty} \sum_{j \in I_t} \mathbb{P}_i(X_t = j)\, p_{j,k} \\ &= \sum_{t=0}^{\infty} \sum_{j \in I_t} (P^t)_{i,j}\, R_{j,k} \\ &= \sum_{j \in I_t} \left[\sum_{t=0}^{\infty} (Q^t)_{i,j}\right] R_{j,k} \\ &= \sum_{j \in I_t} (I-Q)^{-1}_{i,j}\, R_{j,k} \\ &= (NR)_{i,k}. \end{align*}​Pi​(enter recurrent class of k via state k)=t=0∑∞​j∈It​∑​Pi​(Xt​=j)pj,k​=t=0∑∞​j∈It​∑​(Pt)i,j​Rj,k​=j∈It​∑​[t=0∑∞​(Qt)i,j​]Rj,k​=j∈It​∑​(I−Q)i,j−1​Rj,k​=(NR)i,k​.​
