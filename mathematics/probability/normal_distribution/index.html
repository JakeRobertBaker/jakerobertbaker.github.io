Definition · Multivariate Normal#

x∈Rn\mathbf{x} \in \mathbb{R}^nx∈Rn is Multivariate Normal

  ⟺  x∼N(μ,Σ)\iff \mathbf{x} \sim \mathcal{N} \mathbf{ \left( \mu, \Sigma \right) }⟺x∼N(μ,Σ)

  ⟺  \iff⟺ There exists μ∈Rn,A∈Rn×l\mathbf{\mu} \in \mathbb{R}^n, \mathbf{A} \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times l}μ∈Rn,A∈Rn×l such that x=Az+μ\mathbf{x = Az + \mu}x=Az+μ where A⊤A=Σ\mathbf{A^\top A = \Sigma}A⊤A=Σ.

Where z∈Rl,l≤n\mathbf{z} \in \mathbb{R}^l, l\leq nz∈Rl,l≤n is a standard normal vector.
